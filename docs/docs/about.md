# About

Posters.science is a free and open source platform built by the [FAIR Data Innovations Hub](https://fairdataihub.org) for sharing and discovering scientific posters. It is designed to make posters [Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable (FAIR)](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/) and AI-ready so that poster content is ready for search, citation, and reuse.

## Why does this exist?

Millions of scientific posters are presented at conferences every year. They usually contain early-stage findings, novel methods, and emerging results that could accelerate scientific discoveries if made available broadly. Despite this, the vast majority of posters disappear after the conference ends. It often takes years for results presented in posters to appear in journal articles, if they ever do. Estimates suggest that fewer than 20% of presented posters are eventually published as papers, and the average time from poster to publication is around two years.

Posters are not commonly shared directly either. Major repositories like Zenodo and Figshare hold fewer than 52,000 posters combined over the last decade. That is a small fraction of the millions presented during that time. The knowledge in these posters is effectively lost.

Posters.science addresses this gap. By making it fast and straightforward to share a poster with proper metadata, a DOI, and a permanent home in a trusted repository, the platform aims to make poster sharing a routine part of the research process. Posters.science also indexes posters from different platforms to facilitate discovery and reuse.

## Who is this for?

Posters.science is for researchers, conference organizers, funders, and anyone else involved in scientific research:

- **Poster presenters**: Make your poster reusable and citable so your contribution gets credit.
- **Early-career scientists**: Build visibility beyond the conference hall and establish a record of your work from day one.
- **Conference organizers**: Provide presenters a simple way to share their posters and extend the impact of your conference long after the closing keynote.
- **Researchers**: Find early-stage findings to generate new hypotheses, support your ideas, and more.
- **Funders**: Discover what's already been tested at small scale before committing to larger grants since poster findings are often the earliest signal of where research is headed.
