# :key: Creating an Account

To share a poster through Posters.science, you need to sign in with your ORCID account. Searching and browsing the registry does not require an account.

## :gear: Signing in with ORCID

1. **Go to** [posters.science](https://posters.science) and click the `Sign In` button in the top right corner.
2. **Authorize with ORCID**: You will be redirected to ORCID to authorize the connection. If you do not have an ORCID account, you can create one for free at [orcid.org/register](https://orcid.org/register).
3. **Return to the platform**: After authorizing, you will be redirected back to Posters.science and your account will be ready to use.

::: tip
ORCID is a free, unique identifier for researchers. If you do not already have one, creating an ORCID takes less than a minute and is useful well beyond Posters.science.
:::

## :robot: What permissions does Posters.science request?

Posters.science requests read access to your ORCID profile to retrieve your name and ORCID iD. This information is used to pre-fill author metadata when you submit a poster. The platform does not modify your ORCID record.

## :lock: Your data

Your account information is stored securely and used only for the purposes described in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy). You can remove your account at any time by contacting the team at [info@fairdataihub.org](mailto:info@fairdataihub.org).
