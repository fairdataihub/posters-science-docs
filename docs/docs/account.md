# Creating an Account

To share a poster through Posters.science, you need to create an account. Searching and browsing the registry does not require one.

## Signing up

1. **Go to** [posters.science](https://posters.science) and click the `Get started` button in the top right corner.
2. **Fill in your details**: Enter your first name, last name, email address, and choose a password.
3. **Create your account**: Click `Create account` and you are ready to go.

If you already have an account, click `Log in` instead and sign in with your email and password.

![Sign Up Page](/signup-page.png)

::: tip
By signing up, you agree to the [Terms of Service](https://posters.science/terms). Your information is handled according to our [Privacy Policy](https://posters.science/privacy).
:::
