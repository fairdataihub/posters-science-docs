# :rocket: Sharing Posters

Posters.science reduces the process of sharing a FAIR poster to three steps. The goal is to make sharing as fast and painless as possible so that it becomes a natural part of presenting at a conference.

## :memo: The three-step workflow

1. **Upload** your poster file. The platform accepts PDFs, images (PNG, JPEG, TIFF), and presentation files (PPTX, SVG). Drag and drop the file or use the file picker.

2. **Review metadata**. The platform uses AI to read your poster and extract structured metadata: title, authors, affiliations, abstract, funding sources, and more. You review the results, correct anything that needs it, and fill in any gaps.

3. **Publish or download**. Choose to publish your poster directly to Zenodo or Figshare, where it receives a DOI and becomes permanently citable. Or download a structured poster package for your own records.

::: info
You must be signed in to share a poster. See [Creating an Account](./account) for details.
:::

## :watch: How long does it take?

Most posters can be shared in under five minutes. The AI extraction handles the tedious parts: reading author names, pulling out affiliations, identifying funding information. Your job is to verify the results and click publish.

## :link: What happens after publishing?

Once published, your poster is:

- Assigned a **DOI** that you can include in your CV, grant applications, and publications.
- Stored permanently in a trusted repository (Zenodo or Figshare).
- Indexed in the Posters.science registry, making it searchable by anyone.
- Described by structured, machine-readable metadata that follows the [FAIR principles](./fair).
